
# BUILDPRO RECRUITERS — COMPANY MASTER PROFILE

## COMPANY

**Legal/operating name:** BuildPro Recruiters LLC

**Brand:** BuildPro Recruiters

**Founder & CEO:** Lee Chandler

## WHAT WE DO

BuildPro Recruiters is a professional recruiting firm specializing in the built environment.

We connect qualified professionals with companies that need exceptional talent.

## FOUR CORE DISCIPLINES

### Construction
Recruiting professionals across construction operations, project management, field leadership, estimating, development, and executive leadership.

### Architecture
Recruiting architects and architecture professionals across project and organizational levels.

### Engineering
Recruiting engineering professionals across relevant disciplines and levels of responsibility.

### Interior Design
Recruiting interior designers and related design professionals.

## MARKET POSITION

BuildPro is NOT exclusively a C-suite recruiting firm.

The company should serve professionals across multiple career levels while maintaining a premium, highly selective recruiting experience.

The brand should communicate:

**Professional without being intimidating.**

**Premium without being inaccessible.**

**Technology-enabled without losing the human relationship.**

## TWO PRIMARY CUSTOMERS

### EMPLOYERS

Companies that need qualified professionals.

Primary value proposition:

BuildPro helps employers identify, qualify, and connect with talent that fits their specific needs.

### CANDIDATES

Professionals seeking better career opportunities.

Primary value proposition:

BuildPro connects qualified professionals with opportunities aligned with their experience, goals, and expertise.

## CORE DIFFERENTIATOR

BuildPro should emphasize quality over volume.

The recruiting experience should communicate:

- Qualified candidates
- Professional screening
- Industry specialization
- Confidentiality
- Speed
- Personal attention
- Understanding of the built environment
